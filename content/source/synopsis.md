# Out in the Sun

## Synopsis

A contemporary queer romance novel set in Orlando, Florida. *Out in the Sun* follows three couples and the friend group that holds them together through a year of love, old wounds, and hard-won growth. Warm, fun, and occasionally steamy, it's a story of six men, six unique voices, trying to love well and be loved back.

## Main Characters

**Connor** is the heart of the friend group and the one who connected everyone. An Orlando native and a financial analyst, he spent five years building a career in Boston, where he met Tyler, and recently brought him home to Florida. Connor wants the permanence that comes with long-term relationships: he wants the house, the marriage, the joint bank accounts, all the symbols and things established committed couples have. He has been ready for a while. Tyler is not.

**Tyler** is a civil engineer and Connor's partner. A New England native, he met Connor in Boston and later moved to Florida with him and they got an apartment together. Tyler is deeply devoted and in love with Connor, but is scared of the permanence Connor wants: *what if they fall out of love and break up? What if one of them gets sick? What if a job disappears?* The "what ifs" keep Tyler from committing to the permanence that Connor so desires.

**Noah** is a political operative and law school graduate who splits his time between Washington D.C. and Orlando, FL. After the trauma of his ex who was never what he seemed, Noah finds himself falling for Diego, a happy and energetic light that brightens his careful worldview. But the trauma of betrayal still lingers with him, and this hypervigilance may now be fatal for his blossoming relationship.

**Diego** is a college graduate studying to take the LSAT and attend law school. A warm, passionate, energetic person, he has developed an attraction to Noah he has no idea how to be subtle about. They seem to be polar opposites: Noah's careful calculation against Diego's open warmth. But Diego's instinct is to close every gap he sees, and the man he wants might need more room than that.

**Caleb** is a data scientist and sure that no one would ever want him. After losing 180 pounds, Caleb has become a guy whom everyone seems to desire; but this is something Caleb refuses, or is unable, to see. Then he meets Rami, and the wall he built starts to crumble. But will Rami be patient enough for the wall to fully come down?

**Rami** is a social media influencer and small business owner. A gorgeous man, he has always been objectified by every guy he has ever met. But then he met Caleb, who valued him for far more than his looks, and Rami's world turned upside down. Always being pursued, now it's Rami's turn to do the pursuing; but courting Caleb may be the hardest challenge for him yet.
